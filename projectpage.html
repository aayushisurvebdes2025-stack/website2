Erase Me

A haunting journey through fear, memory and the things we cannot escape.

Erase Me is a theatre collateral design project exploring the visual atmosphere of a psychological and haunting narrative. Through a dark visual language, dramatic typography and a limited colour palette, the designs aim to create intrigue and communicate the unsettling mood of the play. The task involved designing two promotional posters, a theatre ticket and a brochure while maintaining consistency across all touchpoints.

Rio's Trip to Space

Bringing stories to life through handmade digital illustrations.

To design and illustrate a children's book for children aged 5–7, using original illustrations created entirely in Adobe Illustrator, without relying on external images.
This project involved developing a visually engaging children's book through original, handmade digital illustrations. Every character, element and scene was created from scratch in Adobe Illustrator, focusing on playful visuals, storytelling and age-appropriate design to create an engaging reading experience for young children.

Storytelling in Frames

The project focused on conceptualising and creating an advertisement for Amul Frozen Parathas around the idea of "Ghar Jaisa". Targeting students and young professionals living away from home, the advertisement uses relatable storytelling and everyday situations to highlight the comfort of home-cooked food. From developing the concept and scripting to storyboarding and visualising scenes, the project explored how to communicate the brand message through an engaging and memorable ad.

Concept in Form

Exploring how time and circumstances shape the different sides of us.

A research-led installation exploring the multiple personalities and identities that coexist within an individual.Content: Inspired by connections drawn from five different sources, this installation explores how human personalities are not fixed but constantly evolving. Mumbaai Chi Local

Stories, struggles and everyday journeys of women travelling through the city's lifeline.

An editorial article exploring the everyday experiences of women travelling on Mumbai’s local trains, highlighting their routines, challenges, shared spaces and unspoken stories.From the morning rush to the evening commute, Mumbai’s local trains carry thousands of women with different destinations but similar experiences. Beyond the crowded compartments lie stories of independence, resilience, friendships and everyday struggles. This article captures the world of women commuters and the unique sense of community they find along the journey.
